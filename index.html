01

Overview

Piyush Water Tracker ("we", "our", or "the App") is a hydration tracking application for Android, developed by NeoYaan Technology. This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights with respect to your personal data.

By downloading and using Piyush Water Tracker, you agree to the terms described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the application.

Piyush Water Tracker is designed to keep your personal data secure. We do not sell your personal information to third parties under any circumstances.
02

Information We Collect

Data you provide directly:

  • Daily water intake logs and hydration records
  • Personal hydration goals and daily targets
  • Reminder times and notification schedules
  • Body weight (if provided for goal calculation)
  • Historical hydration records and progress data

Automatically collected data:

  • Device model and Android OS version (for compatibility)
  • Anonymous crash reports and error logs via Firebase
  • General app usage statistics (features used, session duration)
We never collect your contacts, SMS messages, call logs, location, or any data unrelated to your hydration tracking.
03

How We Use Your Data

The information collected is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Displaying and tracking your daily water intake and hydration progress
  • Calculating personalized hydration goals based on your profile
  • Delivering local notifications and drink reminders at your scheduled times
  • Syncing your hydration data securely via Firebase across sessions
  • Diagnosing technical issues and improving app stability
  • Responding to support requests you initiate

We do not use your data for targeted advertising, behavioral profiling, or any commercial purpose beyond operating the application.

04

Data Storage

Your hydration data is stored using Firebase (Google LLC), a secure cloud platform. Data is transmitted and stored using industry-standard encryption (TLS/SSL). Firebase infrastructure is compliant with major international data protection standards.

Anonymous, non-identifiable crash and usage data may also be transmitted to Firebase Crashlytics to help us improve app stability and performance.

Uninstalling Piyush Water Tracker will remove locally cached data. Your cloud-synced data on Firebase can be deleted by contacting us at the address below.
05

Sharing of Information

Piyush Water Tracker does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share limited data only with trusted third-party service providers solely to operate and improve the app:

  • Firebase Firestore — secure cloud storage for hydration data (Google LLC)
  • Firebase Crashlytics — anonymous crash and error reporting (Google LLC)
  • Google Play Services — app distribution and update delivery

We may disclose information if required by applicable law, court order, or governmental authority, or to protect the safety and security of our users.

06

App Permissions

Piyush Water Tracker requests only the permissions necessary to provide its core functionality:

Permission Purpose
POST_NOTIFICATIONS To send hydration reminders and drink alerts
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM To deliver reminders at your precise scheduled times
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED To restore scheduled reminders after device restart
INTERNET To sync hydration data with Firebase securely
VIBRATE To provide haptic feedback for notifications

You may revoke any permission at any time via Settings → Apps → Piyush Water Tracker → Permissions on your Android device.

07

Children's Privacy

Piyush Water Tracker is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at the address below. We will promptly delete any such information from our records.

08

Your Rights

As a user of Piyush Water Tracker, you have the following rights with respect to your data:

  • Access: View all your hydration data directly within the app at any time
  • Deletion: Delete your data by contacting us or uninstalling the app
  • Opt-out: Disable notifications at any time through Android system settings
  • Portability: Request a copy of your data by contacting us
  • Inquiry: Contact us with any questions or concerns about your data

Your continued use of Piyush Water Tracker following the posting of changes constitutes your acknowledgment and acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
